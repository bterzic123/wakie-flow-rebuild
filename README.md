# Growth Audit — Wakie

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/wakie-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Wakie already has, each built into a clickable prototype of Wakie's own onboarding and paywall. The screens use Wakie's design: the night-city scene, the orange pill, the gender circles, the black paywall with its trial toggle and Sale 60% card. Only the order changed.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 01 | Turn the close alert into a real offer: a full screen showing the 3 free days, then €39.99 for 6 months | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Say what Wakie Plus does on the paywall, led by what this person came for, next to the 4.5 from 19K ratings | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Make "Personalizing Wakie for you" personal: one question, read back on the loader and a "where to start" screen | What brings you · Loader · Payoff | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications after the paywall, behind a screen that says what they are for | Notifications | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Let people in before the sign-in buttons, then ask them to save their profile | Welcome · Sign in | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |

The prototype has English, Russian and Arabic, light and dark, a 30-second walkthrough, and opens full-screen on a phone (`?open=proto`).

## Grounding

Every price, rating, review, member count and Wakie Plus feature comes from Wakie's own App Store and Google Play listings, wakie.com and its in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Wakie.
